# UK discovery rerun evidence — 2026-07-23 source, 2026-07-26 sampler fix

This run reproduces the catalogue recorded in
[`uk-2026-07-23-run.md`](uk-2026-07-23-run.md) from the same pinned source and
replaces its review sample. **The catalogue did not change. Only the sample did.**

## Why it was rerun

The 23 July review sample contained 48 candidates, every one of them a
`good_biking_road`, while the catalogue it was meant to certify has three
layers:

| Layer | In the old sample | In the catalogue |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `good_biking_road` | 48 | 1,100 |
| `twisty_highlight` | 0 | 1,100 |
| `mountain_pass` | 0 | 45 |

Nation stratification worked — 12 each from the England, Scotland, Wales and
Northern Ireland cores — but there was no category stratification. The sample
was ranked by score alone, and because `good_biking_road` scores start from a
higher base than `twisty_highlight` and a mapped pass carries no score at all,
the top 12 in every nation were always good biking roads. Approving that sample
would have published 1,100 twisty highlights and 45 mountain passes on the
evidence of 48 roads from a different layer. Mountain passes are the sharpest
case: seasonal closure is one of the four review criteria and is almost
exclusively a pass concern, so it had no representation at all.

The sampler now stratifies by category as well as nation.

## Determinism

- The catalogue reproduced byte-for-byte from the pinned source, including all
  three layer hashes, so the layer pipeline is unchanged by this fix.
- The review sample is a pure function of the catalogue: rebuilding it from the
  written `discovery-catalogue.geojson` alone reproduces
  `e87778559701f4efd31adfbd7b5c210c4a94b68795d70a4f09724b4e2f76f0f2` byte for
  byte.
- `tools/discovery/tests/` covers sample determinism, independence from input
  feature order, category and nation stratification, quota-shortfall reporting,
  exhaustive pass coverage, and refusal to leave a catalogued category unsampled.
