# France ride readiness

The mobile app recognises France from an offline country-boundary layer rather
than from the phone's locale. A UK phone therefore changes road behaviour when
the ride crosses into France:

- journey distances switch automatically to kilometres unless the rider has
  explicitly chosen a unit;
- mapped posted limits are shown in km/h, while British limits remain in mph;
- roundabout guidance and symbols use right-hand traffic in France;
- route planning, destination search, GPX import/sharing and route-corridor map
  downloads continue to use their existing global providers; and
- camera and police alerts are neither reportable nor presented in France.
  Ordinary hazards such as debris, roadworks and collisions remain available.

Ride Lab uses a 17.9 km excerpt of the supplied Day 3 route to Puy Mary, from
Argentat-sur-Dordogne to Saint-Privat. It includes a country-confirmed French
roundabout and is the quickest way to check the metric and right-hand-traffic
surfaces before travelling.

The last rule is intentional. French government guidance prohibits possessing,
transporting or using a device intended to detect or warn of road-enforcement
controls. See [Service-Public: radar warning devices](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F19459).

## Before leaving

1. Plan or import the complete French route while online.
2. Open the route actions and choose **Download map for offline use** for each
   ride. Route and group state are already offline-first, but destination search
   and creation of a new road-snapped route need connectivity.
3. Start a short test ride with the actual group phones. Confirm kilometres,
   French roundabout direction, group joining and Nearby/internet relay
   behaviour before travel day.
4. Check whether the route enters a low-emission zone and obtain the correct
   Crit'Air certificate in advance. The requirement also applies to motorcycles:
   [French Ministry: Crit'Air](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/certificats-qualite-lair-critair)
   and [low-emission zones](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/zones-faibles-emissions-zfe).
5. Treat roadside signs as authoritative. France's general limits vary with road
   type and conditions, including rain and low visibility; the app displays only
   an explicit mapped limit and does not infer one. The official summary is
   [Sécurité Routière: speed limits](https://www.securite-routiere.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2024-01/sr_vitesse_depliant_2023_bd.pdf).

## Known boundary

The configured live-traffic incident integration remains UK-only. In France the
app continues to provide the planned route, offline maps, turn guidance, group
coordination and rider-reported non-enforcement hazards, but it does not claim a
French live-traffic feed.

The offline road-country layer is generated by
`tools/discovery/generate_road_jurisdictions.py` from Natural Earth 5.1.2. It is
used only for national road conventions; route geometry and roadside signs stay
authoritative.
